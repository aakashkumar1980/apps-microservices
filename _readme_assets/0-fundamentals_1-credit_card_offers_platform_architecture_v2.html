📖 Introduction
🎯 Offer Lifecycle & User Journey

The Credit Card Offers Platform manages the complete lifecycle of promotional offers for cardmembers. Marketing teams create campaigns through the Campaign Management Portal, defining offers such as "10% cashback at Whole Foods" or "3X points on dining". Each offer includes targeting rules, budget limits, and approval workflows.

Eligible cardmembers discover these offers through the Offers Discovery Portal on web and mobile. When a customer enrolls in an offer and makes a qualifying purchase - for example, spending $100 at a restaurant - the Transaction Processing System sends real-time transaction data to our platform. The Transaction Matching Service identifies the match, the Rewards Engine calculates the reward, and the amount is credited to the customer's account.

All events are streamed to the Data Lake via AWS Kinesis for analytics and reporting. Processed data is sent to downstream systems including Statement System, Finance, Compliance, and Customer 360 Platform.

🔗 System Integration

Inbound Integrations: The platform receives data from internal enterprise systems (Core Banking, Transaction Processing, CRM, Marketing Platform) and external third-party systems (Partner feeds, Merchant data). Batch files are received daily for merchant data and customer segments.

Outbound Integrations: The platform calls both internal enterprise APIs (Fraud Detection, KYC/AML verification) and external third-party APIs (Partner Inventory, Credit Bureau, Geolocation, Currency Exchange) through Istio Service Mesh.

⬇️ Downstream Integrations

Processed data is pushed to internal enterprise systems including Statement System, Enterprise Notification Platform, Finance (General Ledger), Compliance & Audit, Enterprise Data Warehouse, and Customer 360 Platform.

📊 Platform Summary

The platform comprises 20+ Spring Boot microservices organized into 6 domains: Campaign & Offer, Customer, Transaction & Redemption, Rewards & Settlement, Merchant & Partner, and Communication. Each domain owns its data in Couchbase NoSQL database.

3 Vert.X high-throughput services handle millions of events daily: Offer Impressions Service (tracks offer views), Click Stream Service (tracks user interactions), and Real-time Transaction Matcher (instant transaction-to-offer matching for push notifications). These services use non-blocking I/O to process 50,000+ events per second at peak.

8 Node.js aggregator services provide optimized API responses for UI portals by combining multiple backend calls into single responses.

6 scheduled batch jobs run during off-peak hours for heavy processing: Eligibility Refresh (nightly pre-computation for 50M+ customers), Offer Expiry (deactivate expired offers and enrollments), Daily Settlement (calculate partner payouts), Report Generation (KPI dashboards and executive summaries), Partner Settlement (weekly invoice generation), and Data Archival (monthly move to cold storage).

Key Technologies: Apache Kafka (event-driven messaging), Couchbase (NoSQL with full-text search), Redis (distributed caching), AWS API Gateway (rate limiting, WAF), Okta (OAuth2/OIDC), AWS Kinesis (event streaming), and Istio Service Mesh (egress proxy with mTLS).

Web applications for internal teams to manage the offers ecosystem. Access is role-based - Marketing manages campaigns, Partner Relations handles partner onboarding, Operations monitors system health, and Customer Service resolves customer queries.

Customer Service agents lookup customer's offers, enrollment status, transaction history, and redemption details. Used to resolve queries like "Why didn't I receive my cashback?" with full audit trail visibility.
🌐 EXTERNAL PORTALS (Customers & Partners)
Customer-facing and partner-facing applications available on web and mobile. Cardmembers browse and enroll in offers, track rewards. Merchants and partners monitor offer performance and settlement data.

🔷 MICROSERVICES (Spring Boot REST APIs)
Backend services implementing business logic following microservices architecture. Each domain is independently deployable, owns its data in Couchbase collections, and communicates via REST APIs (synchronous) or Kafka events (asynchronous). All domain events are streamed to Kinesis for analytics.
📢 Campaign & Offer Domain
Manages the complete lifecycle of marketing campaigns and offers. Handles campaign creation, offer configuration with reward rules, targeting criteria, budget allocation, and multi-level approval workflows before activation.
Campaign Service
CRUD operations for campaigns. Manages campaign metadata (name, description, date range), status transitions (Draft → Pending Approval → Active → Expired), and associations with child offers.
Offer Service
CRUD operations for offers within campaigns. Defines reward type (percentage cashback, fixed amount, points multiplier), qualifying criteria (min spend, specific merchants), and terms & conditions.
Targeting Service
Manages eligibility rules that determine which customers qualify. Rules include customer segments, spend thresholds, card types, geographic regions, and exclusion criteria. Evaluates rules against customer profiles.
Budget Service
Tracks campaign budget allocation and spend. Monitors redemption amounts against budget limits, sends alerts at configurable thresholds (80%, 90%), and can auto-pause campaigns when budget is exhausted.
Approval Service
Manages multi-level approval workflow. Routes campaigns through required approvers (Legal compliance, Finance budget approval, Marketing VP sign-off). Tracks approval status and maintains audit trail.

👤 Customer Domain
Manages customer profiles, eligibility evaluation, offer enrollments, and communication preferences. Aggregates customer data from multiple sources and determines which offers each customer qualifies for.
Customer Profile Service
Aggregates customer data from Core Banking and CRM into unified profile. Contains demographics, card type, account tenure, spending patterns, and segment assignments. Single source of customer context for the platform.
Eligibility Service
Evaluates targeting rules against customer profile to determine eligible offers. Called on every app open to return personalized offer list. Uses cached eligibility results from nightly batch for performance.
Enrollment Service
Manages offer enrollment lifecycle. Creates enrollment record on opt-in, tracks enrollment status (Active, Completed, Expired), handles opt-out, and triggers confirmation notifications via Kafka event.
Preference Service
Stores customer communication preferences. Manages channel opt-in/out (Push, Email, SMS), category interests, and notification frequency settings. Ensures compliance with communication consent requirements.

💳 Transaction & Redemption Domain
Core transaction processing domain. Matches incoming card transactions against enrolled offers, processes qualifying redemptions, and handles customer disputes when rewards are not credited correctly.
Transaction Matching Service
Receives real-time transaction feed. For each transaction, checks customer enrollments, validates merchant/MCC match, verifies amount thresholds, and creates MatchedTransaction record if all criteria are satisfied.
Redemption Service
Processes matched transactions into redemptions. Calls Rewards Engine for amount calculation, creates Redemption record, triggers reward credit via Kafka event, and handles edge cases (partial matches, bonus multipliers).
Dispute Service
Handles customer disputes when expected rewards are not credited. Creates dispute case, investigates by checking transaction and enrollment records, resolves with approval (credit reward) or denial (with explanation).

🏆 Rewards & Settlement Domain
Financial domain handling reward calculations, points balance management, and B2B settlements with partners. Calculates exact reward amounts, manages customer points ledger, and generates settlement files for partner payouts.
Rewards Engine
Calculation engine for reward amounts. Applies offer rules (percentage, fixed amount), promotional multipliers (2X points week), caps (max $50 per transaction), and bonuses (extra $10 for first use). Returns final reward value.
Points Ledger Service
Manages points balance like a bank account. Records credits (earned from offers) and debits (redeemed for gift cards). Tracks point expiry, supports balance inquiries, and handles point transfers between accounts.
Settlement Service
Handles B2B financial settlements with partners. Aggregates redemptions by partner, calculates amounts owed, generates settlement files for Finance, and reconciles discrepancies between our records and partner claims.

⚡ HIGH-THROUGHPUT SERVICES (Vert.X / Java)
Reactive services built on Vert.X framework for high-volume event processing. These services handle millions of events daily using non-blocking I/O and event-loop architecture, achieving 50,000+ events per second at peak with minimal thread usage compared to traditional thread-per-request models.
👁️ Offer Impressions Service
Tracks every offer view event. When a customer sees an offer on their screen (regardless of interaction), the event is captured with customer ID, offer ID, timestamp, and device context. Data feeds analytics for offer visibility and A/B testing metrics.
🖱️ Click Stream Service
Captures user interaction events - clicks, taps, scrolls, and navigation patterns. Tracks "Learn More" clicks, enrollment button clicks, filter selections, and session duration. Powers behavioral analytics and UI/UX optimization.
🔄 Real-time Transaction Matcher
Lightweight real-time matching for instant notifications. When a transaction arrives, performs quick enrollment lookup and basic matching. Triggers immediate push notification ("You just earned $5 at Whole Foods!"). Full redemption processing happens in Transaction Matching Service.

🟢 API AGGREGATOR SERVICES (Node.js / Express)
Lightweight aggregation layer between UI portals and backend microservices. Instead of UI making multiple API calls, aggregator services combine data from 3-5 microservices, apply transformations, and return optimized single responses. Node.js is ideal for I/O-bound aggregation with efficient JSON handling.
📋 Offer Catalog Module
Aggregates offer list for browse screen. Combines Offer Service (available offers) + Eligibility Service (customer-specific filtering) + Merchant Service (merchant details) + Recommendation Engine (personalized ranking).
👤 Customer Dashboard Module
Aggregates home screen data. Combines Customer Profile (summary) + Enrollment Service (active count) + Points Ledger (balance) + Redemption Service (recent activity). Single call replaces 4 backend calls.
🎁 Offer Detail Module
Aggregates offer detail page. Combines Offer Service (full terms) + Merchant Service (locations) + Eligibility Service (qualification status) + Offer Service (similar offers). Includes enrollment CTA logic.
🔍 Search & Filter Module
Handles offer search with Couchbase Full-Text Search (FTS). Supports fuzzy matching, faceted filtering by category/merchant/reward type, and paginated results. Returns results with eligibility status pre-computed.
✅ Enrollment Module
Handles enrollment flow. Validates eligibility, calls Enrollment Service, handles error scenarios gracefully, triggers confirmation notification via Kafka, and returns updated enrollment status for UI refresh.
💰 Redemption History Module
Aggregates reward history screen. Combines Redemption Service (transactions) + Offer Service (offer names) + Merchant Service (merchant details). Supports date filters and CSV export for statements.
🔔 Notification Preferences Module
Manages communication settings. Fetches current preferences from Preference Service, handles toggle updates for channels (Push/Email/SMS), and ensures changes apply immediately to stop unwanted notifications.
🔌 Real-time Updates Module
Manages WebSocket connections via Socket.io for live updates. Consumes Kafka events (new reward earned, offer expiring) and pushes to connected clients. Enables real-time UI refresh without polling.
Tech Stack: Node.js 18+, Express.js, Axios (HTTP client), Socket.io (WebSockets), Redis (response caching), JWT validation
🤖 AI/ML SYSTEMS
Machine learning models powering personalization, prediction, and fraud detection. Models are trained on historical data, deployed as APIs, and called by platform services in real-time. Regular retraining ensures models adapt to changing customer behavior patterns.
💡 Recommendation Engine
Personalizes offer ranking per customer. Uses collaborative filtering and content-based algorithms trained on historical enrollment and redemption data. Returns ranked offer list based on predicted relevance and likelihood to use.
📊 Propensity Model
Predicts probability of offer usage per customer-offer pair. Returns score 0-100 indicating likelihood of redemption. Used by Marketing for campaign ROI estimation and by Eligibility Service for prioritization.
🚨 Fraud Detection
Detects suspicious redemption patterns. Uses anomaly detection trained on historical fraud cases. Flags velocity anomalies (50 redemptions in one day), geographic anomalies, and behavioral deviations for manual review.
👥 Customer Segmentation
Clusters customers into behavioral segments using unsupervised learning. Creates segments like "Premium Diners", "Weekend Shoppers", "Deal Hunters". Used by Marketing for targeted campaign creation.
⏰ BATCH PROCESSING (Spring Boot + Java Threads)
Scheduled background jobs for heavy operations unsuitable for real-time processing. Run during off-peak hours (2-5 AM) using Spring Boot applications with Java ExecutorService for parallel processing. Triggered by Kubernetes CronJobs with monitoring and alerting.
🔄 Eligibility Refresh Batch
Daily job that pre-computes eligibility for all customers against all active offers. Results are cached in Couchbase. Eliminates real-time eligibility computation on app open, reducing latency from seconds to milliseconds.
⏳ Offer Expiry Batch
Daily job that identifies expired offers, updates status to Inactive, transitions related enrollments to Expired status, and archives historical data to cold storage. Maintains database hygiene and query performance.
💰 Daily Settlement Batch
Morning job that aggregates previous day's redemptions by partner, calculates amounts owed based on contract terms, and generates settlement files for Finance. Critical path for partner payment processing.
📊 Report Generation Batch
Morning job generating daily KPI reports - impressions, enrollments, redemptions, reward payouts. Populates data for executive dashboards and sends summary emails to stakeholders.
🤝 Partner Settlement Batch
Weekly job (Mondays) aggregating weekly settlements, generating partner invoices with detailed breakdowns, and creating reconciliation reports. Partners expect weekly settlement summaries for their finance teams.
🗃️ Data Archival Batch
Monthly job moving old data (expired campaigns, old transactions beyond retention period) from Couchbase to S3 cold storage. Maintains operational database performance while preserving data for audit requirements.
⬇️ Stream Events for Analytics ⬇️
🌊 DATA STREAMING & DATA LAKE (AWS)
Event streaming pipeline capturing all platform events for analytics, reporting, and ML training. Operational database (Couchbase) is optimized for transactional workloads. Analytics workloads run on the Data Lake to avoid impacting production performance.
🔥 AWS Kinesis (Managed Streaming)
AWS managed streaming service receiving events from domain producers. Provides durable, ordered event storage with multiple consumer support. Decouples event producers from consumers for independent scaling.
📥 Kinesis Data Streams
Receives events from producers, partitions by key (customer ID), buffers for 24 hours. Multiple consumers can read independently - real-time dashboards, S3 loading, alerting systems.
🚚 Kinesis Firehose
Automatic delivery to S3 without custom code. Batches events (1-5 minute intervals or 1MB), converts to Parquet format, partitions by date, and delivers to Data Lake. Near-zero operational overhead.
⚙️ Kinesis Analytics
Real-time SQL on event streams. Configured for alerting use cases - "Alert if fraud score exceeds 90", "Notify if redemption velocity exceeds threshold". Not for heavy analytics - that runs on Data Lake.
🗄️ Data Lake (Amazon S3)
Scalable, cost-effective storage organized in medallion architecture (Bronze/Silver/Gold). Raw data preserved for reprocessing, transformed data optimized for query performance.
📦 Raw Layer (Bronze)
Data exactly as received - JSON format, no modifications. Source of truth for reprocessing. Partitioned by event type and date: s3://datalake/raw/impressions/2024/01/15/
✨ Curated Layer (Silver)
Cleaned, validated, standardized data. Fixes data types, removes duplicates, handles nulls. Stored in Parquet (columnar format) for efficient analytics queries. Primary layer for analyst queries.
📊 Processed Layer (Gold)
Pre-aggregated, business-ready data. Daily summaries, weekly rollups, KPI calculations. Dashboards query Gold layer for sub-second response times instead of scanning raw events.
🔧 ETL & Data Catalog (AWS Glue)
Serverless ETL service and centralized metadata catalog. Glue Jobs transform data between layers. Glue Catalog provides schema registry for query engines.
🧹 Glue ETL Jobs
Scheduled Python/Spark jobs transforming Raw → Curated → Processed. Handles schema evolution, data quality validation, and partitioning. Orchestrated by Glue Workflows with dependency management.
📚 Glue Data Catalog
Centralized metadata repository. Stores table definitions, column schemas, partition information, and S3 locations. Query engines (Athena, Redshift Spectrum) use catalog to locate data files.
🔥 EMR / Spark
Managed Spark clusters for heavy processing beyond Glue capacity. Used for ML model training on terabytes of data, complex multi-table joins, and one-time data migrations. Spin up on demand, terminate after job completion.
📈 Analytics & BI
Query engines and visualization tools for business users. Different tools optimized for different access patterns - ad-hoc queries, complex analytics, and visual dashboards.
🔍 Amazon Athena
Serverless SQL directly on S3 files. Pay-per-query pricing ($5/TB scanned). Ideal for ad-hoc analysis by analysts - "How many redemptions occurred last Tuesday in California?"
🏢 Amazon Redshift
Columnar data warehouse for complex analytics. Pre-loads processed data nightly. Sub-second query response for frequently accessed reports. Powers dashboards requiring instant refresh.
📊 QuickSight
BI dashboards for business users. Connects to Athena and Redshift. Marketing views campaign performance, Executives view KPI trends, Finance views settlement summaries. Drag-and-drop interface, no SQL required.

⬇️ DOWNSTREAM SYSTEMS (Data Consumers)
Enterprise systems that receive data from our Offers Platform. Data is pushed via real-time APIs, Kafka events, or scheduled batch files depending on consumer requirements and latency tolerance.
📄 Statement System
Generates monthly card statements. Receives reward transaction details to display "You earned $25 cashback from Whole Foods offer". Daily feed with transaction-level data. Late or incorrect data results in customer-visible statement errors.
📧 Enterprise Notification Platform
Centralized email/SMS/push infrastructure. Receives notification triggers with recipient and content details. Handles delivery, retry logic, bounce management. We trigger, they deliver at enterprise scale.
📊 Enterprise Data Warehouse
Central BI repository containing data from all business units. Receives offer activity data for cross-functional analysis - "Do customers using offers have higher overall spend?" Daily batch load with full historical context.
📋 Compliance & Audit
Regulatory compliance system. Receives full audit trail - who was offered what, eligibility decisions with reasons, enrollment/redemption actions. Required for regulatory inquiries proving fair, non-discriminatory offer distribution.
💰 General Ledger (Finance)
Accounting system of record. Receives settlement totals, reward accruals, and revenue recognition entries. Finance month-end close depends on accurate, timely data. Daily batch with reconciliation details.
👤 Customer 360 Platform
Unified customer profile across all touchpoints. Receives offer activity - enrollments, redemptions, preferences. When customer calls support, agent sees complete profile including offer history. Real-time event stream via Kafka.
